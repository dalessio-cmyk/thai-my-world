Thai for Noon

Affection, gratitude, reassurance, playful warmth, apology, and deeper feelings in natural spoken Thai.

Rule for this track

Use her name. With a spouse, “นุ่น” often sounds warmer than repeatedly saying “คุณ.” You usually do not need “ครับ” in intimate lines. “นะ” softens a sentence and often makes affection sound more natural.

iPhone

Install on your Home Screen

Once this app is hosted on HTTPS, install it directly from Safari. No App Store is involved.

Install

1
Open the hosted app link in Safari.
2
Tap the Safari Share button.
3
Choose Add to Home Screen.
4
Turn on Open as Web App if your iPhone shows that option.
5
Tap Add. Launch it from the new Thai icon.
After the first successful load, the lesson app can open offline. Thai speech uses the voices installed on the iPhone and is triggered when you tap “Hear it.”

Back up your progress

Your progress is stored on this iPhone. Export a backup before clearing Safari data or moving to another phone.
Pronunciation uses your device’s Thai speech voice when available. This version is designed around functional spoken Thai, with reading learned through repeated exposure rather than alphabet-first study.
