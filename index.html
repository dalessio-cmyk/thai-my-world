Affection, gratitude, reassurance, playful warmth, apology, and deeper feelings in natural spoken Thai.

Rule for this track

Use her name. With a spouse, “นุ่น” often sounds warmer than repeatedly saying “คุณ.” You usually do not need “ครับ” in intimate lines. “นะ” softens a sentence and often makes affection sound more natural.
